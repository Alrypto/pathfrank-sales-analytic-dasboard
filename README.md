# pathfrank-sales-analytic-dasboard

# Project Overview

I built this **Sales Performance Dashboard in Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales data and turn raw transaction records into clear business insights.

The dashboard allows users to monitor revenue, orders, quantity sold, customer types, product performance, regions, and sales channels. I also added interactive filters so users can explore the data by **month, region, channel, customer type, quarter, and year.**

The goal of this project was to answer important business questions and help management understand **what is driving sales and where there are opportunities for improvement.**

  # Business Problem

A business may have thousands of sales transactions, but raw data alone does not make it easy to understand performance.

The business needs to know:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which regions perform best?
- Which sales channels generate the most revenue?
- How much revenue comes from new vs returning customers?
- Which product categories are performing well?
- How does revenue change from month to month?
- Which products should receive more attention?

This dashboard was created to provide answers to these questions in one interactive report.

# Key Business Insights

From the analysis, I identified several important findings:

## 1. Computers are the main revenue driver

The Computers category generated approximately ₦1.51M, making it the strongest category in the dashboard.

This suggests that the business should continue to monitor demand for computer products and ensure that high-performing products remain available.

## 2. Returning customers contribute significantly to revenue

Returning customers generated approximately ₦1.68M, more than twice the revenue generated by new customers.

This highlights the importance of customer retention and repeat purchases.

## 3. Online sales are performing strongly

The Online channel generated approximately ₦791.54K, making it the highest-performing sales channel.

This suggests there may be an opportunity to invest more in the online sales experience and digital marketing.

## 4. Marketplace sales need attention

Marketplace sales generated approximately ₦310.40K, the lowest among the four channels.

The business could investigate pricing, product visibility, customer experience, promotions, and conversion rates on this channel.

## 5. Regional performance varies

The North and Central regions generated the highest revenue, while the West generated the lowest.

This difference could be investigated further to understand whether it is caused by customer demand, marketing activity, product availability, pricing, or distribution.

## 6. A small number of products generate significant revenue

The Top 5 Products analysis shows that certain products contribute a large share of revenue.

These products should receive close attention when making inventory and promotional decisions.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I would recommend that management:

1. **Prioritize high-performing computer** products to avoid stock shortages.
2. **Strengthen customer retention strategies** because returning customers contribute significantly to revenue.
3. **Invest more in the online sales channel** because it currently generates the highest revenue.
4. **Investigate the low performance of the Marketplace channel** and identify opportunities to improve conversion.
5. **Study the performance gap between regions** and identify what is driving stronger sales in the North and Central regions.
6. **Create targeted promotions for high-performing products** instead of applying the same strategy to every product.
7. **Investigate low-performing categories** to determine whether the issue is demand, pricing, product selection, or marketing.
